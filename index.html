Dear Sis,

Keep shining.

On your birthday, I just want you to know how much your presence means. I hope the year ahead gives you reasons to laugh until your cheeks hurt, dreams that feel exciting, people who genuinely care about you, and peaceful days when you simply feel happy to be yourself.

Life will keep changing, but I hope one thing never changes: that you keep that special place in our family where your smile makes everything feel a little lighter.

Have the most beautiful birthday. You deserve a day that feels as wonderful as the memories you have given us.

With love, your brother ♡
